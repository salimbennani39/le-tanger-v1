In the reception lounge

A private collection of telephones.

From hand-cranked sets to the dials of the 1950s, a collection gathered over the years and displayed at reception. Ask us to tell you its story.

Discover the collection

Late 19th – early 20th century

The hand-cranked wall telephone

No dial yet: you turned the crank to ring the exchange, and an operator, one of the famous “telephone girls”, connected the call by hand.

1950s

The rotary desk telephone

The rotary dial finally made it possible to call without going through an operator. The keys at the front switched between lines, a real office luxury at the time.

1960s – 1970s

The onyx and brass telephone

It borrows the silhouette of Belle Époque handsets. These showpieces took pride of place in living rooms, as much decoration as telephone.
